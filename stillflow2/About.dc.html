Built from proximity to the problem.

Stillflow started from being close to the people who live in one — creators making short-form content, watching them read through script after script, trying to guess which version of a story would actually keep an audience watching.

That's where the real question came from: not "how do we generate more content," but "how do we know which version is worth producing before anyone commits time to it."

Answering that meant going deep into how text and image models actually work together — not as two separate tools, but as one system that can read a script, test its alternatives, and show what each version would look like, all before a single frame is shot.

Stillflow is that system. Built by someone who kept asking the same question the people around them were already living with, until the answer became a product.

The concept worth developing — found before you write the next thousand.
