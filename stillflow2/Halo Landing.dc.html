Stillflow in practice

Use modes

Stillflow is built for teams that need to know what's worth producing before they commit resources to it — starting with the concept stage, and extending into scripts already in development.

Batch generation & scoring

Test hundreds of prompt variants across genre categories at scale, and get a scored, ranked shortlist of concepts worth developing — grounded in volume, not a lucky draw.

Why Stillflow

Not every path gets you there.

Traditional approach

One writer, one instinct, no systematic way to compare against alternatives.

Generic AI tools

Can generate one story at a time — no structured testing across variants, no consistent scoring framework.

Stillflow

Hundreds of variants tested systematically, scored across dimensions, ranked with real statistical grounding.

One lucky generation tells you nothing. A thousand, scored the same way every time, tells you what actually works.

Built from proximity to the problem.

Stillflow didn't start in a writers' room. It started from being close to the people who live in one — creators making short-form content, watching them read through script after script, trying to guess which version of a story would actually keep an audience watching. Answering that meant going deep into how text and image models actually work together — testing them systematically, at scale, instead of trusting a single generation.
