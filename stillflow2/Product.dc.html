How It Works

Testing, systematized.

Stillflow doesn't generate one story and hope. It generates hundreds, scores them consistently, and shows you which patterns actually work.

Four steps — tap a card to see what it returns

01

Define the space

Set the category (horror, rom-com, and beyond) and the prompt variables that matter — tone, scene density, where the twist lands. This is the test design, not a single prompt.

What you get
What you get 01
  • Category: horror, rom-com, and beyond
  • Prompt variables: tone, scene density, twist placement
  • A test design, not a single prompt
  • Variables you choose to hold or vary
Back
02

Generate at scale

Run each prompt variant hundreds to thousands of times. Volume is the point — a single strong generation could be luck. A pattern that holds across a thousand runs isn’t.

What you get
What you get 02
  • Hundreds to thousands of runs per variant
  • Volume is the point, not a single output
  • One strong generation could be luck
  • A pattern across a thousand runs isn't
Back
03

Score every candidate

Each candidate is scored across multiple structured dimensions — literary quality, logical consistency, pacing, hook strength — not a single AI opinion of “is this good.”

What you get
What you get 03
  • Literary quality
  • Logical consistency
  • Pacing and hook strength
  • Structured dimensions, not one AI opinion
Back
04

Get a ranked, evidence-backed shortlist

See which prompt designs, genres, and structures reliably produce strong candidates — and which concepts are worth taking to production.

What you get

  • Ranked candidates, evidence-backed
